Source and accuracy notes
- Blockchain is a broad family of technologies; Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Hyperledger Fabric do not operate identically.
- “Immutable” should not be interpreted as a guarantee that accepted history can never change under any circumstances. Security depends on each network's rules and assumptions.
- A public address does not automatically establish a real-world identity, and blockchain records do not independently prove the truth of off-chain events.
- The IBM Fabric tutorial link previously mentioned was not confirmed as the exact original article URL. The report uses the official project page for the high-level example instead.
